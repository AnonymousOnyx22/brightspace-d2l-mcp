Just built a small MCP for D2L Brightspace because I kept asking my AI "what should I do next" and it would tell me stuff I already handed in.

It connects to your school and checks real submission status, so the to do list only shows what you still need to do. Due dates, grades, course files, announcements all in one place. Sign in once in your browser with your normal school login, no passwords stored.

```bash
npx brightspace-d2l-mcp setup
```

Works at any Brightspace school. Read only. If it saves you from scrolling through five courses to find one missing assignment, that's all I was going for.
